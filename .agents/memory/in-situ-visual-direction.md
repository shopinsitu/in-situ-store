Keep IN SITU image-first and art-directed, combining immersive room photography with warm-ivory catalog compositions, irregular object edits, small figure labels, fine rules, and restrained editorial typography. Preserve the tall IN SITU mark and espresso palette.

**Why:** The user first asked for the visual impact of Freaks, then supplied a more specific direction built around antique room scenes and curated catalog layouts. The newer references supersede the earlier instruction to avoid editorial composition.

**How to apply:** Alternate dark, full-bleed room scenes with spacious ivory sections. Use asymmetrical object groupings rather than conventional product grids where appropriate. Core descriptor: “Art · Objects · Furnishings.” Copy should emphasize curated items with unique personalities and humble intention. References guide the aesthetic only; do not copy their assets or exact layouts.
