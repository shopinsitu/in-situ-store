# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public ecommerce storefront pages, including home, shop, collections, product details, about, and visit/contact content.

## Out of scope
- API endpoints and development-only design/mockup tooling.

## Target audience
- Shoppers and collectors seeking curated antique and vintage furniture, lighting, art, textiles, and decorative objects.

## Primary keywords
- Antique furniture and objects
- Vintage furniture, lighting, and art
- Curated home furnishings

## Crawler assumptions
- Search, social-preview, and AI crawlers should be able to discover and understand public catalog content from the initial HTML response.
